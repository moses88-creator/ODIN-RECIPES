Odin Tacos

Description

These authentic, Mexican-style street tacos are packed with vibrant flavor. Featuring perfectly seasoned, seared flank steak tucked into warm corn tortillas, they are topped simply with crisp diced onions, fresh cilantro, and a squeeze of zesty lime juice.

Simple to prepare yet deeply satisfying, this quick meal delivers the classic taste of a traditional food truck right to your dining room table.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak, diced small
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 finely diced white onion
  • 1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a bowl, toss the diced flank steak with the chili powder, cumin, olive oil, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  2. Heat a heavy skillet or cast-iron pan over high heat. Sear the seasoned steak for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until beautifully browned and cooked through.
  3. While the meat cooks, warm your corn tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat for about 30 seconds on each side until pliable.
  4. Assemble the tacos by layering two warm tortillas together for strength, then spooning a generous portion of meat into the center.
  5. Top each taco with plenty of diced white onion and fresh chopped cilantro.
  6. Serve immediately with lime wedges on the side for squeezing over the top.
